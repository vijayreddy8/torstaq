FAQ

Questions we get a lot.


How long does a website take?

A starter landing page typically takes about a week, and a multi-page business site two to three weeks. Custom applications depend on scope — you'll get a timeline with your quote.

What do the prices include?

Package prices are starting points. Final pricing depends on your requirements and scope, and you'll receive a clear written quote before any work begins.

How does payment work?

We agree on milestones and payment terms in the quote before work starts, so there are no surprises.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. Your domain, hosting accounts and project files are handed over to you at launch.

Do you help after launch?

Yes. TorStaq Care (from ₹2K/month) covers updates, fixes, monitoring and deployment support.

Can you work on an existing site or server?

Yes — redesigns, deployment cleanups, cloud migration and CI/CD setup for existing projects are all welcome.
